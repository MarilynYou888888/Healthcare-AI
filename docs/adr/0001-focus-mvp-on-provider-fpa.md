# Focus the MVP on provider FP&A variance review

Customer discovery exposed a concrete, recurring workflow in U.S. hospital systems and multi-site specialty providers: FP&A analysts investigate facility or market variances, connect them to operational drivers, and decide which forecast assumptions require review. We will therefore build the first product phase around evidence-backed variance explanations and Assumption-Change Proposals for provider FP&A, while retaining the original Healthcare Market Intelligence Copilot as a possible Phase 2 module. The product may recommend retaining, scenario-testing, or reviewing an assumption, but it will not modify an ERP, general ledger, forecast, or budget without an analyst's explicit action.

## Considered Options

- A cross-sector healthcare market-intelligence product spanning providers, payers, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, medical devices, and digital health.
- A pharmaceutical or biotechnology FP&A product grounded primarily in public market, regulatory, and clinical-pipeline events.
- The selected provider FP&A product grounded in recurring internal variance-review workflows described in three customer-discovery interviews.

## Consequences

The MVP must prioritize governed access to financial and operational data, facility-level comparability, evidence provenance, and analyst review. Broad external market monitoring remains valuable but does not define the first product's success criteria or architecture.
